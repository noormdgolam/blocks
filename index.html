Bulk & Project Supply

How Bulk Ordering & Payment Works

A clear, contractor-friendly process from quote to delivery — for every order from 500 pieces to full multi-storey project supply, with payment staged against delivery rather than paid all up front.

  1. 1

    Request a quote

    Send block sizes, quantity and delivery location. We reply with a written quote — unit price, transport and lead time — usually within 2 business hours.

  2. 2

    Confirm quote & advance

    Approve the quote, then a purchase order plus a 30–50% advance reserves a production slot. Approved trade accounts may confirm on a lower advance.

  3. 3

    Production & curing

    Blocks are cast and cured to full 1300 PSI strength. Large orders are batched, so the first delivery can begin while the balance is still curing.

  4. 4

    Scheduled delivery

    Delivery by the truckload, on your site's schedule. Each lot ships against a delivery challan; unloading is arranged by the buyer.

  5. 5

    Balance settlement

    Balance clears per lot or before the final dispatch. Approved developers and contractors can arrange Net 15–30 terms against post-dated cheque or bank guarantee.

Frequently Asked Questions

Expert Answers on Bongshai Concrete Blocks

Last updated: September 2026 · Reviewed by Bongshai Engineering & Construction team.

What compressive strength do Bongshai concrete blocks achieve?

Bongshai concrete blocks achieve 1300 PSI (15 MPa) compressive strength, exceeding BSTI minimum requirements. Suitable for load-bearing walls, boundary walls, and multi-storey infill construction.

Are Bongshai blocks damp-proof and resistant to coastal salinity?

Yes. Bongshai blocks are fully damp-proof and efflorescence-free (ড্যাম্প ও নোনা ধরা মুক্ত). An excellent choice for coastal regions, flood-prone zones, and areas with high groundwater salinity across Bangladesh.

What sizes does Bongshai supply?

Three standard solid sizes, all rated at 1300 PSI:

  • 16″ × 8″ × 3″ (BCB-0101) — partition / non-load-bearing
  • 16″ × 8″ × 4″ (BCB-0102) — general structural / load-bearing
  • 16″ × 8″ × 5″ (BCB-0103) — heavy-duty external / basement

Volume discounts apply for orders of 500+ pieces.

How do I get pricing?

Pricing is provided by quote. Send us the sizes, quantity and delivery location and we reply with a written quote — unit price, transport and lead time — usually within 2 business hours. Prices depend on order size, delivery distance and current raw-material rates.

Where does Bongshai deliver and what is the lead time?

We deliver nationwide across Bangladesh, covering Greater Dhaka, Uttara, Gazipur, and Narsingdi. Typical lead time is 2–5 business days depending on order volume and delivery location.

Are Bongshai blocks compliant with BNBC 2020 standards?

Yes. Bongshai blocks align with BSTI standards and BNBC 2020 guidelines, which actively promote eco-friendly concrete masonry units over traditional burnt red bricks to reduce topsoil destruction.

How does using Bongshai blocks reduce overall construction cost?

Three key savings: (1) up to 30% less mortar due to uniform dimensions; (2) thinner plaster finishing due to flat, precise surfaces; (3) faster masonry (up to 25% quicker) due to larger block coverage per course.

What are the payment terms for a bulk concrete block order?

Once your quote is confirmed, a purchase order plus a 30–50% advance reserves a production slot. For large orders the balance is paid pro-rata against each delivery lot; otherwise the remaining balance is settled before final dispatch. Payment by bank transfer (BEFTN / RTGS), account-payee cheque, or bKash / Nagad for smaller balances.

Does Bongshai offer credit terms to contractors and developers?

Yes. Approved trade accounts can arrange up to Net 30 payment terms against post-dated cheques or a bank guarantee. New accounts start on advance-and-delivery terms and become eligible for credit once a supply history is established.
