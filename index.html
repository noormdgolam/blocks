Frequently Asked Questions

Expert Answers on Bongshai Concrete Blocks

Last updated: September 2026 · Reviewed by Bongshai Engineering & Construction team.

What compressive strength do Bongshai concrete blocks achieve?

Bongshai concrete blocks achieve 1300 PSI (15 MPa) compressive strength, exceeding BSTI minimum requirements. Suitable for load-bearing walls, boundary walls, and multi-storey infill construction.

Are Bongshai blocks damp-proof and resistant to coastal salinity?

Yes. Bongshai blocks are fully damp-proof and efflorescence-free (ড্যাম্প ও নোনা ধরা মুক্ত). An excellent choice for coastal regions, flood-prone zones, and areas with high groundwater salinity across Bangladesh.

What sizes are available and what are the current prices?

Three standard sizes are available:

  • 16″ × 8″ × 3″ (BCB-0101) — ৳40.50 per piece
  • 16″ × 8″ × 4″ (BCB-0102) — ৳50.50 per piece
  • 16″ × 8″ × 5″ (BCB-0103) — ৳60.50 per piece

Volume discounts apply for 500+ piece orders.

Where does Bongshai deliver and what is the lead time?

We deliver nationwide across Bangladesh, covering Greater Dhaka, Uttara, Gazipur, and Narsingdi. Typical lead time is 2–5 business days depending on order volume and delivery location.

Are Bongshai blocks compliant with BNBC 2020 standards?

Yes. Bongshai blocks align with BSTI standards and BNBC 2020 guidelines, which actively promote eco-friendly concrete masonry units over traditional burnt red bricks to reduce topsoil destruction.

How does using Bongshai blocks reduce overall construction cost?

Three key savings: (1) up to 30% less mortar due to uniform dimensions; (2) thinner plaster finishing due to flat, precise surfaces; (3) faster masonry (up to 25% quicker) due to larger block coverage per course.
